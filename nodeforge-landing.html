About the tool

Designed for artists,
engineered for deadlines.

Three workflows that replace hours of node-wrangling with a single click — while keeping every result 100% procedural.

Preset Engine

280+ hand-tuned procedural presets

Every preset is built as a clean, driver-driven node group — metal, glass, fabric, organic, stylized and PBR-heavy. One click inserts the full graph, already organized and annotated.

  • Fully procedural — no baked textures, no external image maps
  • Auto-arranged node groups with color-coded frames
  • UDIM, triplanar and object-projection variants built in
Preset Library Preview clip
Smart Masks

Intelligent masks that follow your mesh

Forget UV-weirdness. NodeForge generates curvature, AO, cavity, height and slope masks with a single node — computed in world or object space and instanced across any geometry.

  • 1-node AO / curvature / cavity / slope masks
  • Voronoi and noise-driven masks with animated seeds
  • Blend, mix and stack masks without a single group input
Mask Engine Preview clip
Baking Studio

Real-time baking, straight from the viewport

Bake any procedural material to PBR texture sets with one panel — no manual image-texture wiring, no UV unwrap guessing. NodeForge sets up the bake targets and slots them back automatically.

  • One-click Albedo / Roughness / Normal / AO bake sets
  • Automatic 8K-safe, padding & margin handling
  • Exports to .png / .exr with colour-management presets

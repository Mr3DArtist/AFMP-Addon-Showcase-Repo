What is Auto Face MoCap Pro

Auto Face Mocap Pro is a all new Flagship Blender Addon that turns any face video into facial animation. Bring a webcam clip, a phone recording or footage you already have, and it detects the performance and maps it onto your character's AR-Kit shape keys (Togue is Not Supported Yet). There is no headset, no suit and no tracking markers to place, only the video you already have (Make sure the video lighting is good and everything is visible clearly).

Everything stays adjustable with real time sliders, so you can tune the result until it looks right, and every slider can be keyframed like any other animation. It supports multiple characters and multiple mocaps Per character, so a whole cast can live in one file.

It is beginner friendly, so even someone on their first day can get a clean result without studying a manual or watching a tutorial, and it all runs locally on your machine, so your footage never leaves your PC and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Creating Shapekeys
Creating Shapekeys

Creating Shapekeys

Creating Shape keys is Easy

Nobody needs to be a rigger to get ARKit shape keys onto a character. There are plenty of free videos on YouTube that walk you through building them by hand, and they work. The catch is time: every key is sculpted and checked one at a time, so a full set can eat an afternoon or more for a single character.

Faceit does that part for you. It creates the ARKit shape keys on your character in about 10 to 15 minutes, so the same character is ready for mocap the day you start instead of next week.

What Faceit Does

Faceit is the Blender add-on that handles the character-preparation side of this workflow. Instead of manually sculpting all 52 ARKit facial shape keys one by one, Faceit gives you a semi-automatic system for generating them specifically for your character.

  • Creates the full set of 52 ARKit-compatible facial shape keys
  • Can reduce hours of manual shape-key work to a much faster setup process
  • Works with an existing compatible face rig, or Faceit can generate its own facial rig
  • Lets you refine individual expressions before baking the final shape keys
  • Prepares the character so those shapes can then be driven by facial motion-capture data

Once everything looks right, Faceit bakes those expressions into the character as the 52 ARKit shape keys.

That's where Auto Face MoCap Pro takes over.

Faceit prepares the face. Auto Face MoCap Pro animates it.

Works With Human Generator

Human Generator gives you a fast way to create the complete character before the mocap stage. Build a customizable human directly inside Blender with the body, face, skin, hair, clothing, and animation rig already set up.

And for facial mocap, the workflow gets even easier.

  • Add Human Generator's facial rig to load the 52 facial shape keys
  • Auto Face MoCap Pro can then drive those expressions using your video mocap

Human Generator builds and rigs the character. Auto Face MoCap Pro brings the face to life.

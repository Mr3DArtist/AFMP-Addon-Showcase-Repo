About
About the tool

Capture a performance,
not a cleanup job.

Seven stages that take you from raw video to final rig animation — with the heavy lifting done automatically.

One-click rig mapping for any face rig

Auto Facial MoCap Pro detects the landmarks it needs, then maps them onto your rig automatically — eyebrows, eyelids, jaw, lips, cheeks and head rotation — without touching your existing bone constraints.

Rig Mapping Preview clip

Track from webcam, video or recorded clips

Capture live from your webcam, batch-process video files, or import footage shot on a phone. The tracker runs offline and works in any lighting once the face is clearly visible.

Tracking Preview clip

Production-ready filters & auto-cleanup

Adaptive smoothing removes micro-jitter, blink detection rebuilds natural eyelid cycles, and built-in limits keep jaw, eyes and head rotation inside believable ranges. Bake to keyframes or NLA strips when you're happy.

Clean-Up & Bake Preview clip

Bake clean drivers & keyframes in one pass

Turn any take into final animation with one click. Auto Facial MoCap Pro bakes tracked motion to shape keys, bone drivers, NLA strips or plain keyframes — keeping your timeline tidy and your takes non-destructive.

Driver Baking Preview clip

Real-time viewport feedback while you record

Watch the rig move as you record. A low-latency viewport preview shows every blink and brow raise live, so you can correct a performance before you even stop recording.

Live Preview Preview clip

Export motion to any pipeline

Ship your takes anywhere. Export clean FBX, JSON or CSV motion data for use in other DCCs, game engines or VFX tools — with per-bone curves preserved.

Export Preview clip

Expression presets for every shot

Dial in a look fast. Packaged expression presets and custom shape-key templates cover everything from subtle dialogue to exaggerated stylized takes — save your own and recall them in one click.
